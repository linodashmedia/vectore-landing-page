Still wondering?

The honest answers to what people ask us before they sign up.

So what is Vectore, really?
It's where you run the whole business: your courses, your coaching, your events, and the people around them, all under one roof. Most tools staple a forum onto a video player and call it community. We started from the opposite end. Your members talking to each other is the heartbeat, and the teaching tools are built around it.
Are you going to take a cut of my sales?
No. Sell a $100 program and $100 lands with you. We make our money from subscriptions, not by dipping into what your students pay. The only cut is the standard processing fee your payment provider (Stripe or Paystack) charges, and that never touches us.
What's this going to cost me?
We're still nailing down public prices, and the people who join now get the best rate we'll ever put out, held for as long as they stick around. The waitlist itself is free: an email, no card, nothing to cancel. You'll know the exact numbers long before anyone asks you to pay.
What can I actually build and sell here?
Just about any shape of teaching. A self-paced course you drip out, a cohort with real start and end dates, coaching one-on-one or in a group, a live workshop, a ticketed event. Bolt on quizzes, assignments, and surveys and you'll finally know who's actually doing the work, not just who bought.
Can I finally drop the other five tools?
For most creators, yes. The scattered kit (a course host here, a community app there, a booking link, a checkout, an email tool, a spreadsheet for the numbers) collapses into one home. Fewer passwords, fewer invoices, and things that finally speak to each other. Attached to one favorite app? Integrations are coming.
When can I get in, and why join early?
We're in private beta and letting people in a batch at a time. Put your name down and you're near the front of the queue. Early folks aren't just first through the door: your price is frozen, the roadmap bends toward what you tell us you're missing, and there's a real human on the other end when you hit a wall.
If I leave one day, do I lose everything?
Your work stays yours, full stop. The lessons you make, the audience you grow, and the revenue you earn belong to you, not to us. Walk away whenever you like and take your members and content with you. No lock-in, no holding your list hostage.
